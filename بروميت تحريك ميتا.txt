استخدم هذه الـ10 كقوالب:

01 — Push In

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen and motionless.

The T-shirt must NEVER move, rotate, deform, fold, stretch, or change shape.

Only the camera moves.

Slow cinematic camera push-in toward the T-shirt.
Start wide and gradually move closer.
Soft premium lighting.
Keep the T-shirt and artwork sharp.
Realistic commercial fashion video.

02 — Pull Out

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen and motionless.

The T-shirt must NEVER move in any way.

Only the camera moves.

Start close to the T-shirt and slowly pull the camera backward.
Gradually reveal the full environment.
Cinematic lighting.
Premium fashion advertisement.

03 — Side Move

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen and motionless.

Do NOT animate the T-shirt.

Only move the camera.

Slowly move the camera from left to right while keeping the T-shirt centered.
Subtle parallax.
Soft cinematic lighting.
Realistic luxury product photography.

04 — Orbit

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen.

The T-shirt must NOT rotate or move.

Only the camera moves.

Slowly orbit the camera around the stationary T-shirt.
Smooth cinematic movement.
Keep the artwork accurate and visible.
Premium fashion campaign.

05 — Low Angle

Keep the T-shirt completely stationary.

Do NOT move or animate the product.

Only the camera moves.

Start from a low angle and slowly raise the camera upward.
Strong premium perspective.
Cinematic shadows.
Luxury streetwear advertisement.

06 — Top Down

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen.

The product itself must never move.

Only move the camera.

Start above the T-shirt and slowly move the camera downward.
Keep the entire product sharp.
Clean cinematic lighting.
Premium commercial photography.

07 — Macro

Keep the T-shirt completely motionless.

Do NOT animate the fabric, artwork, or garment.

Only the camera moves.

Slowly move the camera toward the artwork for a cinematic close-up.
Reveal fabric and print texture.
Shallow depth of field.
Luxury product commercial.

08 — Vertical Move

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen.

The T-shirt must never move.

Only the camera moves.

Slowly move the camera upward from the bottom of the T-shirt toward the top.
Smooth cinematic movement.
Keep the artwork sharp.
Premium fashion lighting.

09 — Focus Pull

Keep the T-shirt completely stationary.

Do NOT move the product.

The camera stays almost completely still.

Create a slow cinematic focus transition from the foreground to the T-shirt artwork.
Subtle lighting.
Realistic lens behavior.
Premium fashion advertisement.

10 — Hero Shot

Keep the T-shirt completely frozen and motionless.

The T-shirt must NEVER move, rotate, deform, or change shape.

Only the camera moves.

Slow cinematic push-in combined with a very subtle side movement.
Finish on a strong hero composition.
Keep the artwork perfectly visible.
Premium cinematic lighting.
Luxury streetwear campaign.
